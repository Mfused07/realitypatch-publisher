RealityPatch Publisher

RealityPatch Publisher is the private publishing tool of the Reality Patch channel, a channel of short animated stories. It lets the channel owner sign in with TikTok and post the channel's own finished videos to the channel's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The channel owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves access.
  2. The tool shows the owner's TikTok account name and the posting options TikTok allows for that account.
  3. The owner picks the video, writes or confirms the caption, and chooses who can see the post.
  4. The tool uploads the video through TikTok's Content Posting API and shows the result.

The tool is used only by the channel owner. It does not post for other people, read other users' data, or collect personal data from viewers.
